  • One uninterrupted U.S.-person period is modeled; pre-residency transactions remain in FIFO/history but are not current U.S. income events. Nonresident U.S.-source/ECI taxation, treaty overrides and repeated residency changes are outside this calculator.
  • Capital gains from dual-basis residency MTM sales are reported separately; complete capital-gain reporting outside Form 8621.
  • External benchmark differences remain disclosed; these worksheets have not been independently certified for filing.
  • Confirmation dates are treated as acquisition/disposition dates under the selected input policy. Verify they represent the tax-effective transaction dates.
  • Treasury quarterly rates are the user-selected approximation, not a claim that quarterly rates equal daily spot rates.
  • Part V annual dividend totals repeat across event worksheets; do not add those repeated totals.
  • Other losses are unnetted and subject to general capital loss, wash sale and other limitations outside this calculator.
  • Fund identity, ownership percentages, taxpayer identity, elections and filing exceptions require completion outside these calculation worksheets.
